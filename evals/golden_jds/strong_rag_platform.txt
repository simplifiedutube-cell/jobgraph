Senior AI Engineer, Retrieval & Agents
Northwind Labs — Remote (India / EMEA friendly)

About the role

Northwind Labs builds a knowledge platform used by ~400 enterprise customers.
Our assistant sits on top of customer document sets that run to hundreds of
thousands of pages, and the quality bar is "a domain expert would agree with
this answer, and can see where it came from."

We are hiring a senior engineer to own retrieval quality and the agent layer
end to end: ingestion, chunking, embedding, ranking, the orchestration that
sits above it, and the evaluation harness that tells us whether a change made
things better or worse.

This is a product engineering role, not a research role. You will ship to
production every week.

What you'll do

- Own the RAG pipeline: ingestion, embeddings, vector search, reranking, and
  the guardrails that keep answers grounded in retrieved context.
- Build and extend multi-step agent orchestration — tool calling, MCP
  integrations, and the control flow that decides when to retrieve, when to
  ask, and when to stop.
- Design the evaluation harness. Define benchmarks for accuracy, latency,
  reliability and cost, and make them run in CI so regressions are caught
  before customers see them.
- Drive down latency and inference cost without giving up answer quality.
- Build the backend services and internal dashboards the team uses to see what
  the system is doing in production.
- Work directly with customers on integration into the systems they already
  run — CRMs, ticketing, internal wikis.

Requirements

- 6+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 2 years building
  production LLM applications.
- Strong Python. You are comfortable owning FastAPI services, Pydantic models
  and SQLAlchemy data access without hand-holding.
- Demonstrated production experience with RAG architectures: embeddings,
  vector databases, chunking strategy, and retrieval evaluation.
- Experience building AI agents with tool use and multi-step orchestration.
- You have built evaluation harnesses before and can talk concretely about how
  you measured whether an LLM system was getting better.
- Experience optimising LLM latency and cost in production.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI/CD.

Nice to have

- Experience with hybrid retrieval (dense + BM25) and reranking.
- Frontend ability — we use React and Next.js for internal dashboards.
- Experience with MCP or similar tool-integration protocols.
- Background integrating with enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, HubSpot).
- Experience with classical ML (scikit-learn) alongside LLM work.

What we offer

Fully remote. Async-first. Competitive compensation, reviewed annually.

Northwind Labs is an equal opportunity employer.
