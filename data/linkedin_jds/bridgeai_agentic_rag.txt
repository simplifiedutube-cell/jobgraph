About the Role
We are hiring a senior, hands-on Agentic RAG Engineer to design, build, and operate the
Retrieval-Augmented Generation platforms that power our autonomous AI agents.
This is a lead-by-example role: you design the architecture, you write the Python, you ship
to production, you mentor engineers by building real systems. You will lead the technical
direction of RAG and agent memory systems, while remaining deeply involved in implementation,
observability, and operational readiness.
GCP is our primary platform, but all designs should be multi-cloud capable.
