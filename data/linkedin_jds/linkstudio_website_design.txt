About Link Studio
Link Studio is a venture studio inside Link Ventures. We co-found AI-native companies from
scratch, building teams around technical talent out of MIT, Harvard, and Princeton.

What we're looking for
A designer who codes. Not a developer who can nudge CSS, and not a designer who hands off a
Figma file and disappears. The bar here is taste and judgment — we're hiring you for what
you'd do differently, not for your ability to execute a spec we hand you.

Required
- A portfolio of sites you designed and shipped. Live URLs, not mockups.
- Strong visual and typographic instincts. Editorial sensibility over template-y
  SaaS-landing-page conventions.
- Comfortable in a modern React/Next.js + Tailwind codebase, and equally comfortable in
  hand-written HTML/CSS/JS when a project doesn't need a framework.
- Git fluency. You'll work in our repos on branches and open PRs.
- Deploys to Vercel.
- Able to work from a rough brief and a conversation.

Nice to have
- Copywriting chops.
- Brand identity work.
- Experience designing for technical/research audiences (ML, deep tech).
- Motion and interaction design used with restraint.
- Light backend: forms, serverless functions, headless CMS, content pipelines.

Not a fit if you need fully specified requirements, work exclusively in Figma, or your
portfolio is mostly WordPress/Squarespace themes.
